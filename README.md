## 🍦 Overview & Brand Experience

**SYMI** (`symi.syahmiaof.my`) is a modern digital flagship web experience crafted for a boutique Mediterranean frozen yogurt brand. The digital experience translates premium hospitality aesthetics into an interactive web journey, fusing high-fashion editorial typography with smooth inertia scrolling and GSAP micro-interactions.

### Key Value Pillars:
- **Functionality**: Horizontal desktop flavor exploration with full keyboard accessibility, touch-optimized swipe controls, and responsive 45/55 editorial layout balancing story and culinary visuals.
- **Aesthetic Excellence**: Mediterranean navy and cream architectural palette, realistic exploded swirl keyframes with real HTML annotations, and Lenis smooth scrolling choreography.
- **Business Impact**: Establishes high brand equity and retail anticipation for modern F&B franchise expansion and direct consumer engagement.

---

## 🎨 Creative Architecture & Interactions

- **Editorial Hero**: Navy Mediterranean architecture, bespoke hero typography, cream action triggers, and tactile benefit badges.
- **Swirl Keyframe**: Exploded culinary photography showcasing artisanal layers (yogurt swirls, sauce ribbons, fresh fruit, granola) overlaid with interactive annotations.
- **Accessible Motion Engine**: Full respect for `prefers-reduced-motion` settings, dynamically removing layout pinning and inertial scrolling while preserving typographic hierarchy.
- **Production Asset Pipeline**: Locally optimized WebP assets with deterministic aspect ratios and zero layout shift.
